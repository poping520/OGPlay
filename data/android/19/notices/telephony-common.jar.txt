This is the unmodified API 19 boot classpath archive from the fixed
android-4.4.4_r2.0.1 build. It contains AOSP frameworks/opt/telephony code and
public telephony classes from frameworks/base. The payload manifest records its
SHA-256 and boot classpath position.
